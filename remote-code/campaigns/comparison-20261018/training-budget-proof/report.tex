\section*{Option specialist and adaptive comparison, 3 bp}
Scope: fixture only.\par
All eight checkpoints and learning-rate choices were frozen before terminal test seed 520261017 was opened. Each domain contains 8192 paired whole episodes; bootstrap intervals use 1000 paired whole-episode resamples.\par
There is one shared training initialization. Intervals describe episode uncertainty conditional on the fitted and selected policies; they do not estimate training-seed uncertainty or prove optimality.\par
The baseline trains on the ordinary/base family only. Adaptive teachers may sample easy/base/hard, so the comparison changes model-family coverage as well as allocation. Specialists are learned comparators.\par
Common learner Adam eps\_root: 1e-16. When 1e-16 is supplied, all final arms use this shared differentiable-Adam migration; preliminary specialist screens remain separate evidence.\par
Reported entropic risks and CVaR95 are finite-sample empirical quantities. CVaR uses fractional boundary mass. Entropic ESS is retained in results.json.\par
No continuous-model convergence or high-default pricing accuracy is inferred from these training/evaluation checks. Numerical claims require the separate supplied pricing audit.\par
Cost, paid-runtime and provider-shutdown evidence will be attached by campaign control after exports and API verification.\par
Policy inputs contain causal price history, time and its own holdings only. Daily marked errors use 256 independent episodes and dealer Q prices including future arrivals; Q price replicate errors are reported separately. Final daily stream 920261017 is distinct from terminal 520261017 and preliminary diagnostics. Finite-sample entropic performance does not establish population exponential-moment integrability.\par
