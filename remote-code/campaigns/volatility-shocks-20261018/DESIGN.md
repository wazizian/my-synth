# Frozen stochastic volatility screen — 2026-10-18

Independent of ongoing credit campaigns. No changes to their jobs or evidence.

Task: terminal replication of one short ATM European call, spot and strike 1 at issue, 126 trading days, zero rates. Stock and cash only, daily holdings constrained to [0,1], proportional transaction costs 1/3/10 bp on traded stock notional including opening and final liquidation. Terminal loss = payoff - initial Q premium - stock gains + costs. The initial premium is a fixed domain-specific shift, excluded during optimization without changing gradients/rankings. Intermediate option marks do not affect terminal wealth. Daily residual metrics use self-financing stock increments less European option mark increments when computed; otherwise daily cash trading errors must be explicitly labeled and not presented as marked hedging errors.

Daily discrete model: two OU factors, eta=.35/sqrt(year), baseline variance .04; v=min(.04 exp(X1+X2),1.0). This explicit variance cap is new, keeps stock conditional variance bounded and its discounted process a true martingale. Each interval uses current factors for the stock return, then exact OU decay/noise and a simultaneous two-factor log(2) jump with Bernoulli probability 1/63. Bernoulli arrival law is part of the discrete model, not an approximation to continuous-time Poisson timing. Easy has no arrivals under either measure. Base/hard have the same positive arrival probability under P and Q; measure changes affect OU drifts only. Each stock increment has conditionally lognormal mean one. Future Q arrivals enter every option price. P half-lives easy/base=(3,3), hard=(40,3); Q half-lives easy=(3,3), base=(40,40), hard=(1,40). Measures have common shock support within each domain. Regime families differ by design.

Each episode starts at option issuance, spot1 and both factors0, followed by126 trading days. The recurrent price history grows causally from issuance; there is no pre-issuance warmup. This fixes the initial Q premium within a domain and avoids silently dropping a path-dependent initial premium from the entropic objective. All arrivals are unannounced and random; there is no fixed-date event during training. Diagnostic event plots condition on no previous arrival and an arrival at day35, with ordinary future arrivals thereafter; prices still include future Q arrivals. This conditional event study is separate from unconditional training/evaluation.
